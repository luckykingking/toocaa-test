Prepare a Useful TOOCAA Support Request

Collect the machine, software, material, settings, symptoms, and photos that help TOOCAA support understand a fault.

Identify the machine and problem

Include the exact model, laser module, relevant accessory, and order or serial information through the official support channel. Describe what you expected, what happened, and whether the fault is repeatable or intermittent.

Record the job configuration

List material identity and actual thickness, the file type and size, power, speed with units, passes, processing mode, focus method, and software version. Note the firmware only if you can read it through the normal supported interface.

Show the fault safely

Take a clear photo of the sample and any displayed error. A short video can show unexpected motion when it is safe to record. Do not recreate a dangerous event, bypass an interlock, or expose the beam to make a better recording.

Summarize the checks already completed

List each check and its result rather than saying everything was tried. Include whether the same file works on another blank and whether the fault began after a stock, software, accessory, or maintenance change.

Prepare your support notes

Use this checklist to draft a request locally. Copy the result and send it through TOOCAA's official contact page. Nothing is sent from this form.

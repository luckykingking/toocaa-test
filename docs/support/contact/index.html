Contact TOOCAA Customer Support

Need help with your machine, an order, or a project? Contact TOOCAA at service@toocaa.com or use the contact page to reach the team.

Contact the support team

Email service@toocaa.com or use the TOOCAA contact page for product, order, and technical questions. For setup instructions and machine manuals, open the TOOCAA Support Center.

For a machine issue

Have the exact model, module, material, software version, speed units, job settings, and a clear description ready. Include a photo of the sample or error when safe and relevant. Send order and serial information only through the official private support channel.

For a buying question

Describe the material, object size, intended cutting or engraving operation, workspace, and required accessory. That lets the product team discuss a concrete application without relying on a broad claim about all materials.

While a fault is unresolved

Leave the machine stopped if a safeguard, damaged component, unexpected motion, persistent flame, or abnormal smoke condition is involved. Follow the manufacturer's instructions before returning it to use.

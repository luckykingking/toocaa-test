Laser Cutter Maintenance & Inspection

Inspect the bed, optics, extraction route, and moving parts using the maintenance instructions for the installed machine.

Power down before access

Stop the job, let the work area cool, and disconnect power before accessing components as directed in the manual. Do not reach into moving parts or perform electrical repair from a general cleaning guide.

Inspect optics and the bed

Check for deposits or damage on accessible optics and remove loose scraps from the working bed. Use only the cleaning materials and access method specified for the component. An unsuitable solvent or wipe can damage an optical coating.

Check extraction and movement

Inspect accessible hoses, ducts, and filters for blockage, poor seating, or damage. Check the work area for obstructions and follow model-specific mechanical inspection steps. Do not tighten belts or lubricate rails by guesswork.

Verify after maintenance

Reassemble protective parts, restore the documented focus and configuration, and run a small baseline sample. Record the work and any replaced components. If a fault remains or a component appears damaged, stop and request support.

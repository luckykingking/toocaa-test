TOOCAA Setup After Delivery

Check the delivery, identify your exact model and module, complete the official setup, and keep a record of the first test.

Check the delivery before assembly

Compare delivered components with the packing list and inspect for visible transport damage. Record the order and serial details. Contact support about damaged or missing components before using a setup that may be incomplete.

Follow the correct manual

Identify the exact machine and laser module, then follow its assembly and protective-system instructions. Plan extraction and clearances before powering up. Avoid instructions for another model that happen to show similar components.

Connect and verify

Install the supported software from the official source. Confirm the device, workspace, origin, and speed units. Test movement and framing as directed by the manual before sending the first processing job.

Keep the first sample

Use a simple file and documented stock. Note the settings, focus method, and software version, and retain the sample. It provides a baseline if a later material batch or maintenance change affects the result.

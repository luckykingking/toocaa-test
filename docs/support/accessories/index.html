Choose Accessories for TOOCAA L2 & NOVA

Compare camera, rotary, air assist, working-panel and purifier choices for TOOCAA L2 and NOVA, with model-specific product and manual links.

Start with the machine, not the accessory name

L2 and NOVA have different camera, rotary and module arrangements. Match the exact accessory product and its supported machine before ordering. A similar connector or a photograph of a related product is not a compatibility statement.

Choose around the actual project

For flat signs and cut panels, prioritize a suitable work surface, documented stock and the prescribed smoke-extraction setup. For a cylindrical object, choose the correct rotary and confirm how the object will be held. A handle, taper or soft wall can change the supported setup even when the nominal diameter looks suitable.

Camera and focus are different functions

L2's optional Smart Camera has its own calibration instructions. NOVA's built-in view uses its own Studio calibration and autofocus workflow. A camera can help position artwork but does not make an unknown material safe or guarantee that a warped sheet stays in focus.

Plan the airflow system as a whole

Air assist at the workpiece is different from enclosure smoke extraction. A purifier is not permission to engrave unknown plastic indoors, and its cartridge is not the same part as NOVA's washable rear machine filter. Read the accessory maintenance instructions before selecting replacement filters or deciding where the machine can operate.

Send a clear compatibility question

When a listing does not state that your exact configuration is supported, send TOOCAA the model, installed laser module, accessory product name and intended material/object dimensions. Ask which mounting parts, cables or filters are required. Do not purchase on a guessed fit or use an adapter to force an incompatible part to work.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the L2 rotary instructions for NOVA?

No. NOVA has a distinct rotary and X-axis installation. Match both the accessory and the machine manual.

Does a purifier make every material safe indoors?

No. Identify and approve the material independently, and follow the machine and purifier ventilation instructions.

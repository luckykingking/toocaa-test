Make Personalized Gifts with a TOOCAA Laser

Prepare names, dates, photos and small gift sets with a clear design check and a test on matching stock before engraving the final blank.

Plan the finished project

Choose a blank whose complete composition is documented. A wooden tag, leather label, coated-metal keepsake and stone photo panel need different preparation. Confirm whether the design needs an outline cut, text engraving or a photograph before preparing the file.

Projects to start with

Choose a small representative project before committing to a collection or batch.

ProjectPreparationInspect
Names and datesWood tags or suitable identified blanksSpelling, punctuation and actual text size
Photo keepsakesDocumented wood or stone engraving blanksCrop, contrast and readable tones
Gift packagingKnown paper, cardboard or woodFit, fold direction and assembly

Work through one complete sample

Keep the process and the finished result together so a later change can be compared.

  1. Confirm every name, date and image permission.
  2. Preview the design at the final physical size.
  3. Test on matching inexpensive stock.
  4. Inspect placement and finish after cooling and cleaning.

Choose the setup for the work

A built-in camera can help place a design, but it cannot restore an old overlay after the blank moves. Refresh the view and check focus using the model guide. Round gifts may need a compatible rotary, not a flat-bed setup with guessed height or diameter.

Check before the next job

Keep the material identity, artwork version, device, module, focus method and job settings with the retained sample. Check text, placement, dimensions, edges and intended handling after cooling and any documented finishing. A new material batch or fixture deserves a fresh sample rather than relying on the old photograph.

Frequently asked questions

Can the same setup be reused after the material changes?

Recheck composition, thickness, focus, the matching setting and a small sample. A retained artwork file is not proof that a new batch will process identically.

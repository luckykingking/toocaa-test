Laser Cutter Safety Before Every Job

Check materials, ventilation, workholding, and protective safeguards before cutting or engraving with a desktop laser.

Know what is going under the beam

Identify the complete material, including coatings, adhesive, backing, and treatments. Do not process PVC, vinyl, unidentified plastics, or stock whose composition cannot be confirmed. A settings entry for one material does not approve a different material with a similar appearance.

Work through the job

Use the following sequence with the current instructions for the exact machine. Keep the first job small enough to inspect clearly and leave time to check the result before repeating it.

  • Confirm the material is documented for the machine and process.
  • Clear scraps and flammable clutter from the working area.
  • Check extraction, the exhaust path, and any filters according to the manual.
  • Use the supplied protective system and keep interlocks functional.
  • Frame the job and stay present throughout processing.

Know when to stop

Stop the job for persistent flame, unusual odor, unexpected smoke leakage, loss of motion, or a safety warning. Use the machine's documented stop procedure. Do not bypass sensors or keep retrying a job with an unresolved safety fault.

Save the setup for the next job

Keep the artwork version, stock details, software version, laser module, focus method, speed units, power, passes, and a photo of the finished test together. If any of those change, run a fresh check rather than assuming the old result still applies.

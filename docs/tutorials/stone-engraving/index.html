Stone & Slate Engraving Preparation

Level a stone blank, prepare high-contrast artwork, and compare a small engraving before making coasters or keepsakes.

Start with a known blank

Stone varies in mineral composition, flatness, texture, and sealant. Use an inexpensive blank from the same batch as the final piece. A rough or curved surface may be out of focus across part of the image, even if the central area is correct.

Work through the job

Use the following sequence with the current instructions for the exact machine. Keep the first job small enough to inspect clearly and leave time to check the result before repeating it.

  • Identify the stone and check for coatings, cracks, and loose layers.
  • Support the blank level and set focus to the engraving surface.
  • Prepare simple text or high-contrast artwork.
  • Run a small test using model-specific guidance.
  • Clean and inspect the result before considering a finish.

Choose detail the surface can show

Start with bold text or line art before testing a photograph. Compare the result at the intended viewing distance. Texture can hide subtle gray detail; increasing image resolution alone will not create contrast that the material cannot produce.

Save the setup for the next job

Keep the artwork version, stock details, software version, laser module, focus method, speed units, power, passes, and a photo of the finished test together. If any of those change, run a fresh check rather than assuming the old result still applies.

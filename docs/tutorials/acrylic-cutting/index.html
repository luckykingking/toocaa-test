Acrylic Cutting With a Diode Laser

Check acrylic compatibility, select a matching published setting, and inspect a small cut before making signs or jewelry.

Color and wavelength matter

Transparent or pale acrylic can behave very differently from dark acrylic under a diode laser. Start by checking the machine's documented material compatibility. Do not rely on a tutorial for a different laser technology or assume a color change is only cosmetic.

Work through the job

Use the following sequence with the current instructions for the exact machine. Keep the first job small enough to inspect clearly and leave time to check the result before repeating it.

  1. Confirm that the sheet is acrylic and suitable for the laser wavelength.
  2. Identify thickness, surface masking, and any backing.
  3. Choose a documented row for the installed module and material.
  4. Frame a small sample with extraction operating.
  5. Inspect the cooled edge, underside, and dimensions.

A useful quality check

Compare a straight edge, a small hole, and a corner. If the sample melts or fails to separate, stop and check compatibility, focus, and support before increasing exposure. Save the settings alongside the exact color and supplier of the acrylic.

Save the setup for the next job

Keep the artwork version, stock details, software version, laser module, focus method, speed units, power, passes, and a photo of the finished test together. If any of those change, run a fresh check rather than assuming the old result still applies.

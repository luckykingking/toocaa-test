Unusual Noise from a TOOCAA Laser Machine

Locate an unfamiliar rattle, scrape or fan noise without running an unsafe test or opening powered components.

Applies to: TOOCAA L2 / TOOCAA NOVA

Record when the noise happens

Note whether the sound begins at power-on, during homing, when the head changes direction, when air assist starts, or only while cutting. The timing helps distinguish motion, cooling and airflow sources. A new sound after installing an accessory deserves attention even if the first engraved mark looks normal.

Inspect after stopping and powering down

Only inspect accessible areas covered by the user documentation. Keep the machine stopped if a component is loose or damaged.

  1. Check the bed for scraps touching moving parts.
  2. Inspect external exhaust tubing for contact that could transmit vibration.
  3. Check workholding and accessories for a loose or shifted mounting.
  4. Look for dirt around accessible fan openings without inserting tools into the fan.

Distinguish a fan problem from a movement problem

A noise that changes with head direction is not the same as a fan rattle that occurs while stationary. NOVA has a specific cooling-fan cleaning guide; its procedure and access do not automatically apply to L2 or an external purifier. Identify the actual source before choosing a maintenance guide.

When the machine should stay off

Stop for grinding, a stalled axis, a fan that does not run normally, an electrical smell, or a damaged guard. Do not repeatedly run the machine to collect a better recording. Tell support which operating stage produced the sound and what changed immediately beforehand. Do not open a power supply or lubricate an unidentified component to silence it.

Frequently asked questions

Does every unusual noise mean the belts need tightening?

No. Scraps, workholding, ducts, fans and motion faults can sound different. Identify when and where it occurs before considering a documented adjustment.

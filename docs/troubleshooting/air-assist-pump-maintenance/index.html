Air Assist Pump Care & Airflow Checks

Inspect accessible pump intake, tubing and output without opening electrical housings or assuming a filter can be washed.

Applies to: TOOCAA L2 / TOOCAA NOVA

Identify the pump and its controls

Record whether the machine uses NOVA's smart air-pump controls or an L2 accessory configuration. The control method and service parts may differ. Also identify whether a suspected filter belongs to the pump, the enclosure exhaust or an external purifier; those are different systems.

Inspect with the equipment off

Stop processing, switch off and disconnect the relevant power supplies before access.

  1. Look for accumulated dust at accessible intake areas.
  2. Inspect the external tube for bends, pinching, loose seating or visible residue.
  3. Check that the tube route has not been changed by a module or rotary installation.
  4. Use the accessory's own instructions for removing an intake element or replacing a service part.
  5. Do not blow debris deeper into the pump or put liquid into an air line.

Use condition and the accessory instructions

NOVA's FAQ identifies pump operation and blocked or bent tubing as causes to check when marks burn abnormally. It does not establish that every pump has the same replaceable filter or cleaning interval. Match the exact accessory before ordering parts, washing an element or copying another pump's service schedule.

Check normal airflow before another cut

After documented maintenance and correct reconnection, verify the intended control mode and normal airflow without exposing the beam or reaching into moving equipment. If output stays absent, the pump overheats or noise is abnormal, stop and contact support. Do not open a powered pump, replace wiring or compensate for missing air with more laser exposure.

Frequently asked questions

Should I wash the air-pump filter like the NOVA built-in exhaust filter?

Not unless the exact pump instructions permit it. The washable rear machine filter and a pump intake element are not interchangeable maintenance items.

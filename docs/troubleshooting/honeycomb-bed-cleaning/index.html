Clean the Laser Working Bed & Remove Debris

Clear scraps and residue from the correct working panel without burning deposits away or damaging an accessory bed.

Applies to: TOOCAA L2 / TOOCAA NOVA

Check which working panel is installed

A blade cutting panel, an optional honeycomb panel and the surface below it have different construction. NOVA's rotary guide identifies a blade cutting panel and aluminum protective plate, not a universal honeycomb bed. Use the maintenance instructions for the actual installed part.

Clear loose debris with power off

Stop the job, allow the workpiece and panel to cool, switch off and disconnect power.

  1. Remove finished parts and loose offcuts without touching a moving or hot component.
  2. Check underneath the working panel for small pieces that could lift stock or interfere with airflow.
  3. Use the component's documented removal method if access requires taking out a panel.
  4. Inspect for damage or residue that affects flatness before reinstalling it.

Choose cleaning materials for the panel

Remove deposits using the method and cleaning product approved for that panel. Do not flood the machine, soak electrical parts, scrape a coated bed aggressively or assume that a bonded honeycomb insert is washable. Keep solvent vapors and flammable supplies away from processing, and reconnect power only after cleaned components are dry and correctly installed.

Check a flat reference after reassembly

Make sure the panel is seated properly and no scrap is trapped beneath it. Verify a flat offcut before making a detailed job. L2's safety guide identifies accumulated residue and debris as a fire risk, so cleaning should not wait for a visibly scorched bed. Never run the laser over the panel to burn deposits off as a cleaning shortcut.

Frequently asked questions

Can I clean a dirty bed by running a burn-off pattern?

No. That adds heat and fire risk to accumulated residue. Clean the stopped, cooled component using its approved maintenance method.

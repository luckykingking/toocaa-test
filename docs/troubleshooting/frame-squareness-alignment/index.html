A Cut Square Is Not Square: Geometry Checks

Compare imported artwork, cut measurements and workpiece placement before attempting a machine-frame adjustment.

Applies to: TOOCAA L2 / TOOCAA NOVA

Check the design dimensions first

Open a simple square in the design software and confirm equal side dimensions at the final physical size. A skewed photograph, image trace or imported drawing can look square without containing square geometry. Confirm the imported size instead of relying on how it fits the screen.

Measure a simple cut sample

Use a small flat test piece only when the machine is safe and moving normally.

  1. Check that the stock is fixed and does not rotate during processing.
  2. Inspect the file for duplicate paths or a distorted import.
  3. After cutting and cooling, measure both pairs of sides and both diagonals.
  4. Record whether the error repeats in the same direction on another safe sample.

Rule out measuring and material effects

Measure the same feature each time, rather than comparing one charred edge with another clean edge. Check for bowing, broken corners and loose assembly joints. If only an assembled box is skewed but the individual panels are square, inspect the tab allowance and assembly sequence before blaming the machine frame.

Frame adjustment needs the correct procedure

Do not loosen the gantry, move limit switches or force rails into position using a general re-alignment suggestion. L2 assembly hardware and NOVA's enclosed structure are not the same service operation. Repeated unequal diagonals on sound simple samples should be sent to support with the original file, side and diagonal measurements, and any transport or collision history.

Frequently asked questions

Are unequal box diagonals proof that the laser frame is bent?

Not by themselves. Verify the drawing, individual cut panels, measurement points and assembly fit before requesting a frame inspection.

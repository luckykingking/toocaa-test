NOVA Camera Preview Does Not Match the Engraving

Check lighting and workpiece focus, then use TOOCAA Studio's NOVA camera-calibration workflow to correct a persistent overlay offset.

Applies to: TOOCAA NOVA

Separate an overlay offset from a moving blank

Compare the preview with the actual workpiece without repositioning the material. A consistently displaced camera overlay differs from a blank that slips or a machine that loses position. Check focus for the real material thickness and refresh the overlay before deciding calibration is needed.

Prepare the calibration sheet

The official workflow uses one clean, flat sheet of kraft paper from the NOVA material kit. Place it in the working area while the machine is powered off. Remove other objects and avoid direct sunlight or strong lighting that produces washed-out areas in the camera view. Keep the lid closed during the calibration process.

Run the NOVA calibration in Studio

Connect the machine successfully and use the current on-screen wizard.

  1. In the upper-left TOOCAA menu, open Laser Tools > Camera Calibration.
  2. Continue to the framing stage and confirm that the framed area is fully covered by the paper.
  3. Keep the lid closed while framing and autofocus complete.
  4. Start the calibration-pattern engraving and do not move the paper.
  5. Allow recognition and calibration to finish, then inspect a refreshed overlay.

Check the result before the next finished piece

The official guide says NOVA adjusts the camera view for material thickness after autofocus. Routine thickness changes are not a reason to repeat calibration every time. Recheck after moving the machine, replacing camera-related parts or noticing a persistent offset. If the offset remains after correct calibration, send support a preview screenshot and a photo of the corresponding sample; do not loosen the camera mounting to compensate by eye.

Frequently asked questions

Must I calibrate again for every material thickness?

NOVA's camera guide describes thickness compensation after autofocus. Recalibrate for a persistent offset or a relevant machine/camera change, not automatically for every sheet.

Laser Head Skipping, Grinding or Losing Position

Stop a grinding or skipping movement, check visible obstructions, and distinguish an artwork-boundary problem from a motion fault.

Applies to: TOOCAA L2 / TOOCAA NOVA

Stop the movement first

A head that chatters, stalls or moves in steps should not be left to finish the job. Stop using the normal machine controls. Let the machine stop completely and disconnect power before inspecting the working area. Do not reach into a moving gantry or attempt to hold the head in position.

Look for a simple obstruction

Check the area you can inspect without disassembling the motion system.

  1. Remove loose offcuts, raised workholding and objects in the travel path.
  2. Check that the material and any rotary workpiece fit within the documented clearances.
  3. Inspect visible cables or tubing for snagging without pulling connectors.
  4. Look for displaced covers or a component loosened during a recent setup change.

Check the file and coordinate setup

A design outside the device work area or an incorrect origin can send a frame movement toward a boundary. Confirm the selected machine profile and work area before another attempt. A file correction is appropriate only when the mechanics are unobstructed and the machine is otherwise ready.

Do not tune the driver by guesswork

Motor-driver adjustment is a model-specific service operation, not a general user repair. Do not change motor current, hidden controller values, belt tension or limit-switch positions from a generic guide. A persistent noise or lost position after visible obstructions are removed needs support with the exact axis, movement direction, setup change and a safe recording of the original symptom.

Frequently asked questions

Can I continue if the head moves again after skipping?

Lost position can misplace the remaining toolpath. Stop and address the cause before starting a new framed job; do not resume a damaged alignment by eye.

NOVA Autofocus Fails or Reports No Material

Distinguish a focusing-probe problem from material recognition and follow the NOVA focus-module checks before retrying.

Applies to: TOOCAA NOVA

Read the exact focusing message

NOVA's FAQ connects a No material detected message after focusing with a probe that may be stuck or obstructed. That is not the same as the camera failing to identify a material recognition card. Record the message and whether the head moved, touched the workpiece, or stopped before reaching it.

Check the stock and the focus area

Stop and disconnect power before access. Let the laser module cool. Check for a tilted blank, a loose surface layer or a fixture under the probe path. Do not repeatedly drive the head against an object to force detection.

Inspect the Focus Module

Use the illustrated NOVA module instructions for the installed module.

  1. Check for dust or residue around the magnetic Focus Module and probe.
  2. Keep the probe from being pressed sideways, bent or used as a handle.
  3. If removal is needed, support the module and follow the correct power-off removal sequence.
  4. Reinstall the Focus Module fully and verify that no debris prevents it from seating.
  5. Stop if the probe remains stuck, rusty or damaged rather than adding an unapproved lubricant.

Use the focusing method appropriate to the workpiece

The NOVA rotary manual distinguishes autofocus for hard workpieces from the documented manual height method for soft ones. An irregular or compressible object may not behave like a flat rigid sheet. After correct reassembly, check focusing on suitable test stock; if the error returns, keep the machine stopped and send the exact symptom and module details to support.

Frequently asked questions

Does No material detected always mean the camera cannot recognize the material?

No. The NOVA FAQ identifies the focusing probe as a relevant cause when the message appears after focusing. Material-card recognition is a separate workflow.

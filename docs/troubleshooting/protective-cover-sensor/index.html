Protective Cover or Guard Warning on TOOCAA

Check normal lid and guard installation, identify a recurring interlock warning, and keep safeguards intact during diagnosis.

Applies to: TOOCAA L2 / TOOCAA NOVA

Read the warning before moving parts

Record whether the warning appears during startup, framing or processing. L2's alarm reference identifies two short beeps with opening the shield during laser processing. NOVA's FAQ includes lid closure, module installation and bottom-cover alignment in initialization checks. These are different model contexts, not one interchangeable sensor procedure.

Check ordinary closure

Stop the machine before inspecting the enclosure.

  1. Remove stock, cables or fixtures that physically prevent the lid or protective part from seating normally.
  2. Check for visible damage, loose hinges or displaced panels without disassembling the sensor.
  3. If an accessory required a panel to be removed, reinstall the protective arrangement specified by that accessory's manual.
  4. Do not push harder against a lid that is obstructed or distorted.

Keep magnets and wiring out of the test

Do not tape a switch shut, add a magnet to simulate closure, bridge a connector or operate with a protective part removed. Those actions can make an unsafe machine appear ready while leaving the underlying fault unresolved. A damaged or unreliable enclosure should remain out of use until repaired.

When normal closure still gives a warning

Send support a photograph showing the actual cover position, the model, accessory setup and full message. Mention whether the issue began after transport, cleaning or an accessory installation. Do not repeat laser-output tests with an unresolved interlock. The useful result is reliable operation with all guards normally installed, not merely clearing the warning once.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use an external magnet to check a cover sensor?

Do not use a sensor bypass to make the machine operate. Check normal closure and ask support for the exact diagnostic procedure if the warning remains.

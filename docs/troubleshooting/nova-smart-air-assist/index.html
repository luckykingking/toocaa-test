NOVA Air Assist Has Weak or No Airflow

Check air-pump operation, accessible tubing and job context when NOVA air assist is absent or not behaving as expected.

Applies to: TOOCAA NOVA

Separate air assist from smoke extraction

Air assist supplies air near the processing area; the exhaust path removes smoke from the enclosure. A running exhaust fan does not prove that the air pump is delivering air, and a dirty exhaust filter is not the only possible cause of a directional burn mark.

Record the behavior during the job

NOVA is described as having smart air assist. Its behavior can depend on the operation and job configuration, so note whether the problem appears during framing, engraving or cutting. Do not expect an idle machine to reproduce every airflow condition of a processing job.

Inspect accessible tubing safely

Stop, switch off and disconnect power before handling air lines or reaching into the enclosure.

  1. Look for a kinked, pinched or displaced accessible tube.
  2. Check for visible debris at the accessible air outlet without inserting a sharp tool.
  3. Confirm that a recent accessory or module change did not disturb a connection.
  4. Use the module and accessory instructions for any removal or cleaning.
  5. Keep liquids away from the air path and electrical parts.

Do not keep cutting without resolving the fault

The NOVA FAQ specifically identifies pump operation and bent or clogged tubing as checks for burn marks. If air remains absent, the pump is noisy or hot, or cut quality changes sharply, stop and report the operation mode, settings, module and visible tube condition. Do not open the pump housing, bypass control electronics or use a higher power setting as compensation.

Frequently asked questions

Is the exhaust fan the air-assist pump?

No. They serve different parts of the airflow system. Identify which airflow is missing before choosing a maintenance or support procedure.

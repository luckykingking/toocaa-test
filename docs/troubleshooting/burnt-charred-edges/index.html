Burnt Edges & Smoke Stains

Identify excess dwell, duplicate lines, flatness problems, and smoke deposits before changing the cutting workflow.

Identify the symptom

A dark edge and a smoke-stained face are different observations. Check whether the line is being cut more than once, whether the sheet is level, and whether smoke is being removed from the work area.

Checks in order

Begin with the visible status, configuration, and material. Keep a note of each check and its result. Inspect hardware only with the machine stopped and power disconnected as directed by the manual.

  • Inspect the file for duplicate vectors and repeated passes.
  • Compare power, speed units, and operation with the material reference.
  • Check focus, support, and any documented air-assist setup.
  • Inspect extraction and remove scraps with power off.
  • Compare one small sample after changing a single factor.

Stop and seek support when needed

A surface mask must itself be compatible with the laser process. Do not add an unknown adhesive or coating to hide a setup problem, and stop for persistent flame.

What to include in a support request

Send the exact model and module, software version, material identity, file details, job settings with units, and the full error message. Describe the last successful job and any recent change. Include clear photos when safe; do not reproduce a hazardous event to record it.

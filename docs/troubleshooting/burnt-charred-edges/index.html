Burnt Edges & Smoke Stains

Identify excess dwell, duplicate lines, flatness problems, and smoke deposits before changing the cutting workflow.

Applies to: TOOCAA L2 / TOOCAA NOVA

Identify the symptom

A dark edge and a smoke-stained face are different observations. Check whether the line is being cut more than once, whether the sheet is level, and whether smoke is being removed from the work area.

Separate air-assist and exhaust checks

The NOVA FAQ names excessive parameters and a pump or tube problem as checks for burn marks. The pump that supplies air is not the enclosure exhaust fan. Inspect tubing and the relevant filter separately, and do not wash an optional purifier cartridge using the built-in machine-filter instructions.

Checks in order

Begin with the visible status, configuration, and material. Keep a note of each check and its result. Inspect hardware only with the machine stopped and power disconnected as directed by the manual.

  1. Inspect the file for duplicate vectors and repeated passes.
  2. Compare power, speed units, and operation with the material reference.
  3. Check focus, support, and any documented air-assist setup.
  4. Inspect extraction and remove scraps with power off.
  5. Compare one small sample after changing a single factor.

Stop and seek support when needed

A surface mask must itself be compatible with the laser process. Do not add an unknown adhesive or coating to hide a setup problem, and stop for persistent flame.

What the check tells you

Choose the next step from the observed result, keeping a record of what actually changed.

Observed resultNext step
A broad surface haze surrounds the cutCheck smoke deposits and extraction separately from kerf depth.
The same outline is cut twice unexpectedlyRemove duplicated geometry or unintended layer output.
Burn marks are strongly directionalCheck the documented air-pump and tubing operation before another cut.

What to include in a support request

Send the exact model and module, software version, material identity, file details, job settings with units, and the full error message. Describe the last successful job and any recent change. Include clear photos when safe; do not reproduce a hazardous event to record it.

NOVA Material Recognition Gives the Wrong Result

Check the TOOCAA material recognition card, camera view and selected job instead of treating automatic settings as proof of material safety.

Applies to: TOOCAA NOVA

Know what the recognition workflow reads

TOOCAA describes NOVA's automatic material settings as a camera workflow using material recognition cards or tags and the Studio material library. It is not a chemical analysis of any unknown sheet placed in the machine. Confirm the supplier's material identity even when a suggested preset appears.

Check the view and the selected material

Before processing, compare the suggestion with the material you actually intend to use.

  1. Check that the appropriate recognition card or tag is present and visible as directed by the product workflow.
  2. Remove glare, overexposure and objects hiding camera reference points.
  3. Confirm the selected laser module, operation and material thickness.
  4. Refresh the camera view and compare the applied layer settings with the intended job.
  5. Do not process a sheet whose composition or backing cannot be identified.

Separate recognition from focus and calibration

A No material detected message after autofocus may concern the focus probe. A marker-point warning or a displaced overlay concerns the camera view and calibration. Use the corresponding guide rather than changing presets to suppress an unrelated message.

Treat a preset as a starting reference

For documented stock, inspect power, speed units, passes and operation before a small controlled sample. If the card and material do not match, use the correct documented material workflow or ask support. Do not label unknown plastic as acrylic, or copy a setting for another module simply because the automatic suggestion is missing.

Frequently asked questions

Does automatic recognition make an unknown plastic safe?

No. Identify the complete material and any coating or backing independently. A camera suggestion is not a safety assessment or approval to process PVC or an unknown composite.

Slide-Rod Cleaning & Lubrication

Remove residue from the correct motion surfaces and use only the lubricant and access method approved for the installed machine.

Applies to: TOOCAA L2 / TOOCAA NOVA

Identify the motion surface first

NOVA's documentation calls these slide rods. Do not assume that every shiny shaft, belt, wheel, bearing or enclosure surface should be greased. Identify the machine and the maintenance item before choosing a product. A generic spray recommendation is not a substitute for an approved lubricant.

Clean and distribute the specified grease on NOVA

The NOVA slide-rod guide describes the following maintenance sequence, with power disconnected and the module cool.

  1. Remove dust, tar and old lubricant from the slide rods using compatible cleaning materials.
  2. Apply the lubricating grease specified for the component.
  3. Move the axis evenly as allowed by the guide to distribute the grease; stop for resistance.
  4. Wipe away excess rather than leaving a heavy layer to collect debris.
  5. Inspect smooth movement and belt condition before reassembly and normal operation.

Do not invent a replacement product or schedule

The linked guide does not name a universal retail grease or a fixed service interval for every work environment. Use the supplied or specifically approved product, and ask TOOCAA if its specification is unclear. Keep lubricant away from optics, belts, electrical contacts and the working stock. Do not use a general-purpose solvent spray as an assumed substitute.

Keep a simple maintenance record

Record the date, product specification and observed condition. Recheck when a reminder appears or residue and uneven movement return. L2 users should follow L2's corresponding maintenance instructions rather than transferring the NOVA access procedure. Persistent rough travel or a recurring mechanical noise belongs with support, not another unverified coating of grease.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use any household lubricating spray?

No. Use a lubricant explicitly approved for the particular motion component and keep it away from optical and electrical parts.

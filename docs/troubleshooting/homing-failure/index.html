Homing Failure or Unexpected Motion

Stop unexpected movement, check visible obstructions and configuration, and gather useful evidence for support.

Identify the symptom

Homing failure may present as a blocked axis, repeated travel into a limit, a grinding sound, or an incorrect origin. Record what the head was attempting to do and whether a fixture, cable, or accessory was recently changed.

Checks in order

Begin with the visible status, configuration, and material. Keep a note of each check and its result. Inspect hardware only with the machine stopped and power disconnected as directed by the manual.

  • Stop the machine using the documented procedure.
  • Disconnect power before inspecting the work area.
  • Check for a visible obstruction, loose item, cable snag, or misplaced fixture.
  • Review the selected machine profile and accessory configuration.
  • Follow the model's documented recovery instructions or request support.

Stop and seek support when needed

Do not force an axis while powered or repeatedly drive into a stop. Avoid changing firmware travel limits or sensor configuration to conceal an unresolved fault.

What to include in a support request

Send the exact model and module, software version, material identity, file details, job settings with units, and the full error message. Describe the last successful job and any recent change. Include clear photos when safe; do not reproduce a hazardous event to record it.

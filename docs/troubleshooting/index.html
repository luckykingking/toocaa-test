TOOCAA Troubleshooting: Cutting, Connection & Safety

Find symptom-based checks for TOOCAA laser output, incomplete cuts, engraving quality, movement, connection, smoke, safety alerts, and firmware.

For a damaged safeguard, persistent flame, unexpected motion, or abnormal smoke condition, stop the job and follow the model-specific safety instructions before further checks.

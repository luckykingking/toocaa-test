Restore NOVA Factory Settings Safely

Use the documented reset for the right symptom, record your configuration and verify reconnection before returning to a job.

Applies to: TOOCAA NOVA

Know why you are resetting

The NOVA FAQ describes Restore Factory Settings for certain display or Wi-Fi problems and says a full restart is required afterward. A reset is not a general repair for blocked airflow, a damaged focus probe, misinstalled hardware or an interrupted update. Diagnose that category first.

Record what you will need again

Before resetting an idle, responsive machine, save the editable project and record the machine identity, firmware version, network details and job configuration. Keep screenshots of relevant settings. A screenshot record is not a promise that there is an automatic export-and-restore feature for every calibration or preference.

Follow the normal settings-menu route

Use Restore Factory Settings in the machine's settings menu as described by the FAQ. Allow the device to restart fully before deciding that the reset failed. If the screen cannot reach that menu, ask support for the specific recovery procedure rather than inventing a button combination.

Recheck the working setup

After the restart, reconnect using the documented NOVA workflow. Confirm the selected device, Wi-Fi information where needed, installed module, focus behavior and current overlay. Recalibrate the camera if alignment is visibly wrong, then use a small suitable test. Do not immediately rerun a saved customer job with unverified settings or repeat resets indefinitely when a fault returns.

Frequently asked questions

Will a reset fix every NOVA warning?

No. It does not replace hardware installation checks, cleaning or a correct firmware-recovery procedure. Use it for the symptom covered by the official guidance and verify the setup afterward.

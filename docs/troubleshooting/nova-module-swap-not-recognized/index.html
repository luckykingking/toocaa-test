NOVA Does Not Recognize the Laser Module

Check compatibility, seating, fasteners and normal startup after a NOVA module change without forcing the connector or bypassing detection.

Applies to: TOOCAA NOVA

Confirm the module and the symptom

Record the installed module identity and whether NOVA reports no module, fails initialization or shows a different warning. A compatible product name is not enough if the part, mount or firmware requirement differs. Do not apply the 10W/20W window-maintenance procedure to an infrared module without its own matching instructions.

Power off before checking the mounting

The NOVA 10W/20W maintenance guide describes supporting the cooled module, removing its two securing screws and sliding it horizontally. Use its illustrations for the actual locating features.

  1. Stop, switch off, disconnect power and allow cooling.
  2. Check that the correct module is fitted and its mounting area is free of debris.
  3. If reseating is needed, follow the illustrated removal/reinstallation sequence and support the module.
  4. Stop if the module resists sliding into position; withdraw and realign rather than forcing it.
  5. Secure the module and check that the Focus Module is fully seated before power is restored.

Check the protective parts too

The NOVA FAQ identifies the lid and bottom cover as part of normal initialization checks. Reinstall protective parts correctly after maintenance and clear all tools from the enclosure. Do not run the machine with a loose module or an open connector simply to see whether detection changes.

If detection still fails

Keep the machine stopped and send support the old/new module identities, error screen and the setup change that preceded it. Do not scrape contact pins, bend connectors, install unrelated firmware or override module detection. A damaged mount or electrical contact needs the correct service response rather than another forced insertion.

Frequently asked questions

Should I push harder if the module will not seat?

No. The official guide says to stop and realign when resistance is significant. Forcing the mount can damage locating features or connectors.

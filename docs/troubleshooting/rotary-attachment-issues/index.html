Rotary Attachment Slipping or Misalignment

Check accessory compatibility, support, grip, clearance, and the documented rotary configuration before another test.

Applies to: TOOCAA L2 / TOOCAA NOVA

Identify the symptom

Tapered objects, handles, uneven grip, and unsupported weight can make a cylindrical object slip or move out of focus. Check the physical support before correcting a distorted design with repeated software scaling.

The two rotary systems are different

L2's 5-in-1 rotary guide does not replace the NOVA rotary instructions. NOVA uses the 90-degree X-axis setup, its own connections and the workpiece diameter at the actual processing area. Confirm the accessory and module before transferring any configuration between machines.

Checks in order

Begin with the visible status, configuration, and material. Keep a note of each check and its result. Inspect hardware only with the machine stopped and power disconnected as directed by the manual.

  1. Stop and power down before changing accessory connections.
  2. Verify the exact rotary and machine are compatible.
  3. Check support, level, grip, and clearance for the object.
  4. Review the official device and rotation configuration.
  5. Use an inexpensive sample for a small alignment check.

Stop and seek support when needed

Stop for a stalled drive, slipping object, collision, or cable snag. Restore the documented normal configuration before returning to a flat-bed job.

What the check tells you

Choose the next step from the observed result, keeping a record of what actually changed.

Observed resultNext step
The accessory is not detectedCheck its exact model, power-off connection procedure and supported device configuration.
The mark wraps to the wrong widthCheck the measured diameter at the engraving area, grip and configuration.
The setup is NOVA rather than L2Use NOVA's 90-degree X-axis rotary guide instead of the L2 installation.

What to include in a support request

Send the exact model and module, software version, material identity, file details, job settings with units, and the full error message. Describe the last successful job and any recent change. Include clear photos when safe; do not reproduce a hazardous event to record it.

Laser Overheating Warning or Cooling Problem

Stop for a temperature warning, check the relevant cooling and exhaust paths and avoid an unsupported timed restart.

Applies to: TOOCAA L2 / TOOCAA NOVA

Stop and preserve the warning

Record the exact temperature or cooling message, processing settings and stage of the job. A hot laser module, an exhaust problem and a hot external purifier involve different components. Do not assume that a warning clearing briefly means the machine is ready to continue a long cut.

Check the environment and ventilation

L2's safety guide specifies its permitted operating environment; use that model reference rather than assuming the same limits for every product. Keep cooling and exhaust openings unobstructed. Do not place the machine among scraps or loose coverings that interfere with air movement.

Inspect after shutdown and cooling

Disconnect power and let the laser module cool completely before cleaning.

  1. Inspect accessible cooling-fan areas for dust, residue or damage.
  2. Keep module cooling separate from the enclosure exhaust and any optional purifier.
  3. Follow the exact component cleaning guide and use compatible tools.
  4. Do not insert tools into a fan, wash electrical parts or apply liquid directly to the module.

Resume only after normal cooling is established

The NOVA fan guide calls for stopping when the fan remains abnormal, noisy or unable to cool properly after cleaning. Do not invent a fixed waiting time or disable a temperature sensor. A recurring warning, a fan that does not run normally, unusual odor or damaged wiring needs support before processing resumes. Report which component appears affected and whether the warning returns on a small normal test.

Frequently asked questions

Can I restart after waiting a fixed number of minutes?

There is no universal cooldown time for an unresolved fault. Allow cooling and verify the cause and normal operation using the model-specific instructions.

Excessive Smoke or Odor

Stop processing and check material suitability, extraction, ducting, and filters when smoke is not being managed normally.

Identify the symptom

Different materials produce different residues, but visible leakage or an unusual odor should not be treated as an expected result to tolerate. Identify what changed and keep the machine stopped while the cause is unresolved.

Checks in order

Begin with the visible status, configuration, and material. Keep a note of each check and its result. Inspect hardware only with the machine stopped and power disconnected as directed by the manual.

  • Stop the job and follow the manufacturer's safe stopping procedure.
  • Confirm the complete material is suitable for laser processing.
  • With power off, inspect accessible ducting and hoses for damage or blockage.
  • Check seating and maintenance of filters using the model instructions.
  • Resume only when the documented extraction arrangement is restored.

Stop and seek support when needed

A filter is not a universal approval to process unknown materials. Stop immediately for persistent flame or an unsafe air condition, and request manufacturer guidance when the fault remains.

What to include in a support request

Send the exact model and module, software version, material identity, file details, job settings with units, and the full error message. Describe the last successful job and any recent change. Include clear photos when safe; do not reproduce a hazardous event to record it.

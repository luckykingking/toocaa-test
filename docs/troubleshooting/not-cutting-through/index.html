Laser Not Cutting Through

Check material identity, thickness, focus, speed units, and support when a laser cut leaves attached fibers or an incomplete outline.

Identify the symptom

A failed cut is not always a power problem. A different adhesive, a bowed sheet, duplicate paths, or an unsupported material can change the result. Inspect where the cut failed before choosing another setting.

Checks in order

Begin with the visible status, configuration, and material. Keep a note of each check and its result. Inspect hardware only with the machine stopped and power disconnected as directed by the manual.

  • Confirm the material and operation are documented for the installed module.
  • Measure actual thickness and inspect flatness, coatings, and glue layers.
  • Compare the selected reference row and software speed units.
  • Check focus, extraction, and workpiece support.
  • Run a small controlled sample and inspect the underside after the machine stops.

Stop and seek support when needed

Stop if the sample overheats, flames persist, or the material produces unexpected fumes. Do not keep burning a stuck area or leave additional passes unattended.

What to include in a support request

Send the exact model and module, software version, material identity, file details, job settings with units, and the full error message. Describe the last successful job and any recent change. Include clear photos when safe; do not reproduce a hazardous event to record it.

Blurry or Wavy Engraving

Check focus, surface stability, artwork quality, and machine motion when engraved lines look soft or uneven.

Applies to: TOOCAA L2 / TOOCAA NOVA

Identify the symptom

Look at both a straight vector and a small filled area. If the source image is already soft, machine adjustments cannot recover the missing detail. If sharp vectors become wavy on the material, inspect the setup and motion.

Compare camera clarity with the engraved result

A blurred NOVA camera image may need top-cover camera cleaning and calibration; a sharp overlay with wavy vectors points elsewhere. Do not change the physical camera mounting to conceal a motion problem. Keep a vector sample and a small filled sample for comparison.

Checks in order

Begin with the visible status, configuration, and material. Keep a note of each check and its result. Inspect hardware only with the machine stopped and power disconnected as directed by the manual.

  1. Confirm the file is sharp and correctly scaled.
  2. Level the surface and set focus according to the module guide.
  3. Secure the workpiece and check the fixture for movement.
  4. Inspect accessible moving parts with power disconnected.
  5. Compare a small baseline file before making any documented mechanical adjustment.

Stop and seek support when needed

Belt tension, rail lubrication, and calibration procedures differ by model. Avoid arbitrary adjustments; document abnormal noise or play for support.

What the check tells you

Choose the next step from the observed result, keeping a record of what actually changed.

Observed resultNext step
Only the on-screen NOVA camera picture is blurredUse top-cover camera inspection and cleaning, not motion calibration.
The source image is blurredPrepare a sharper original at the intended size.
A simple vector becomes wavy on the workpieceStop for an unresolved motion or workholding problem.

What to include in a support request

Send the exact model and module, software version, material identity, file details, job settings with units, and the full error message. Describe the last successful job and any recent change. Include clear photos when safe; do not reproduce a hazardous event to record it.
